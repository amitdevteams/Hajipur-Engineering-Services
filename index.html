What we do

Maintenance & repair for every part of the forecourt

From the submersible pump in the ground to the dispenser at the nozzle, we service the equipment that keeps a petrol pump compliant and operational.

Fuel dispenser servicing & repair

Routine servicing, flow-meter calibration, nozzle and hose replacement, and breakdown repair for dispenser units.

Submersible pump maintenance

Installation, inspection and repair of submersible pumps and motors that draw fuel from underground storage tanks.

Underground tank calibration

Dip-stick and tank chart calibration to keep stock measurement accurate and audit-ready.

Electrical & electronic systems

Wiring, control panels, totalizers and indicator boards — repair and preventive checks to avoid downtime.

Safety compliance & inspection

Equipment checks aligned with fire and safety norms, ahead of licensing renewals and inspections.

Annual maintenance contracts

Scheduled upkeep on a yearly contract, so equipment issues get caught before they become breakdowns.
